# Getting help

This page is for **people who use ReadyNow**. If you are here to work on the
code, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) instead.

## If you are in an emergency right now

**GitHub cannot help you.** Nobody monitors this site around the clock, and
anything posted here is public and permanent.

Use the app to send your alert. For legal help, contact a legal services
provider directly.

## If you need help with the app

Email **<innovationlab@humanrightsfirst.org>**.

Please **do not open a GitHub issue** to ask for help with your account, your
emergency plan, your contacts, or a message that did not arrive. Issues on this
site are public, indexed by search engines, and cannot be reliably deleted once
posted. Email reaches the same people privately.

When you write to us, it helps to say:

- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- whether you are on an iPhone or an Android phone
- the app version, shown on the About screen

You do not need to include your phone number, your contacts, or anything about
your immigration situation, and we would rather you did not unless it is
genuinely necessary to answer your question.

## What we cannot do

We build and maintain the app. We are not your lawyers, and we cannot give
legal advice, represent you, or tell you what to do in your case. ReadyNow is a
tool for reaching people you have chosen in advance — it is not a substitute
for legal representation.

## Reporting a security problem

If you have found a vulnerability, do not email general support and do not open
an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting a conduct problem

Harassment or other Code of Conduct violations go to
<innovationlab@humanrightsfirst.org>. See
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Suggesting an improvement

If you have an idea and you are comfortable posting in public, open a
[feature request](https://github.com/Human-Rights-First-Innovation-Lab/readynow/issues/new/choose).
If a translation reads wrongly in your language, that is one of the most useful
things you can tell us — there is a template for it.
